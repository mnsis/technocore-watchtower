INTERPRETATION

Scanner signal guide

DID_PRESENT
A did:key identifier was observed in normalized metadata. Watchtower did not independently verify its signature.
UNSIGNED_PRIVILEGED_NAME
A privileged-looking display name was observed without signed identity metadata.
POTENTIAL_TECHNOCORE_WRITE_URL
Static URL structure matched a documented write-capable route. The URL was not contacted.
SUSPICIOUS_COMBINATION
Multiple independent risk indicators occurred together. This is not proof of malicious intent.
